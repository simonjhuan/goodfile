Support

GoodFile transfers photos, videos, documents and text directly between your devices over your local Wi‑Fi or hotspot. Files never pass through a server or the cloud.

Need help or want to report a problem? Email us and we usually reply within 2–3 business days.

Getting started

Send a file

  • Open the Send tab and tap Send a file.
  • Pick a photo, video or document (or take a new photo/video).
  • Let the other device scan the QR code, or enter the 4‑digit code shown.

Receive a file

  • Open the Receive tab.
  • Android / Windows: show the QR code / 4‑digit code to the sender.
  • iPhone / iPad: tap Open Direct Receive and scan the sender’s QR code for a direct device‑to‑device transfer.

Troubleshooting

  • Devices can’t find each other: make sure both are on the same Wi‑Fi network, or use one device’s hotspot and connect the other to it.
  • Transfer stalls or fails: some public / guest / corporate Wi‑Fi networks block device‑to‑device traffic ("client isolation"). Try a home network or a phone hotspot.
  • Windows: allow GoodFile through Windows Firewall when prompted (it needs local ports 8080–8081).
  • Camera or QR scan won’t open: grant Camera permission in your device Settings for GoodFile.
  • Received file not visible: check your Downloads / Documents folder, or the in‑app History tab.

Privacy

GoodFile does not collect personal data. Transfers happen directly between your devices on your local network. The app does not upload your files to any server. An optional ad banner may appear after 15 days of use.
